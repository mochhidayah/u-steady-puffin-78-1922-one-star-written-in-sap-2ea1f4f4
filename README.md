A tiny review composer: a houseplant scores three invented one-star
drafts of its human owner — plant-world detail, a concrete grievance,
a punchline after the dash — and writes the winning line to
`/output/entry.txt` (one line, under 280 characters).

Run it:

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The winning line:

> One star: he repotted me into a souvenir mug with no drainage, calls it minimalist, and now I live ankle-deep in my own bathwater like a teabag someone forgot — at least the mug says 'World's Best Dad'.
